# 2.1.0 — In Development

Companies come to BankSystem: found a company, hire workers, mint physical shares, pay dividends and recurring wages, and watch it all on a live statistics dashboard.

## Performance

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![improvement] | Bank autosave interval is now seconds-based, default 5 seconds (was minutes, default 5 minutes) — closes an item-duplication window where a server crash after a withdrawal but before the next save left items in both the bank and the player inventory. Setting renamed `SAVE_INTERVAL_MINUTES` → `SAVE_INTERVAL_SECONDS`, bounds `[1, 86400]`. **Migration note:** existing `settings.json` files with the old key fall back to the new 5-second default; servers with custom intervals must re-set them in seconds. |

## API

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Companies — new top-level entity with chunked-NBT persistence and an immortal-founder invariant</summary><br>Introduces the `Company` object (immutable name and max supply, mutable description, founder set, bound bank account) and `CompanyManager` (case-insensitive unique-name registry, three-index in-memory lookup, chunked NBT under `data/BankSystem/Companies/`, load-drop when the bound account is missing, duplicate-name-on-load keeps the older record). Founders cannot lose MANAGE on the bound bank account — enforced through a founder-aware overload of `ServerBankAccount.enforceManageInvariant` and a new `REFUSED_FOUNDER` outcome wired into `UpdateBankAccountRequest`. Creating a company seeds the founder with `DEPOSIT`, `WITHDRAW` and `MANAGE` on the bound account. The failed-subsystem backup sweep (`DataHandler.backupFailedSubsystemData`) covers the `Companies/` folder, so a failed boot never leaves stale company data on disk.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>`AsyncCompanyManager` — full async request surface for company operations across master and slave servers</summary><br>Every company operation forwards from a slave to the master through the same async pattern as `AsyncBankManager`. Slaves never touch company data on disk, and error paths return typed result codes that render identical chat feedback on both sides. The function set covers company lifecycle (create, transfer founder, dissolve, update description, info), rights-scoped company listing for tab-completion, payout management (create / update / pause / delete / list / history) behind a MANAGE gate, dividend runs, share-visual updates, symbol-library sync, StockMarket market control, and read-only queries — company by account, company info by id, holder count, share visuals by id, list all company visuals, and company statistics (cashflow series, headline totals, top shareholders, days-to-insolvency estimate). Read-only queries are allow-listed for untrusted slave servers; every mutation is re-checked on the master.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Transaction Ledger — new database table capturing every deposit, withdrawal and transfer</summary><br>Introduces `TransactionLogRecord`, `TransactionLogManager` and the `sql/TransactionLog.sql` schema (indexed on `(account_number, ts DESC)` and `(company_id, ts DESC)`). Write hooks fire on deposit, withdrawal, banknote withdrawal, bank terminal deposits, and both legs of a transfer (source `TRANSFER_OUT`, destination `TRANSFER_IN`). Ledger kinds `PAYOUT`, `DIVIDEND`, `SHARE_STAMP`, `SHARE_REDEEM` and `SHARE_TRADE` classify company activity. The read API exposes `getByAccount`, `getByCompany`, `getByAccountAndKind` and `getCount`. Writes are master-only; the database layer was generalised from a single hardcoded schema path to a list of schema resources.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Recurring and one-time payouts — company-scoped worker schedules with audit trail and per-tick scheduler</summary><br>Introduces `PayoutSchedule` (id, target UUID, amount, currency, interval, mode, next-run tick, paused flag, created at / by) persisted on `Company` NBT alongside a `nextScheduleId` allocator. Two modes are supported: recurring, and `ONE_TIME`, which fires once at the scheduled time and then deletes itself from the company. `PayoutExecutor` ticks every 20 server ticks, iterates every company, resolves the target's personal bank for the chosen currency — auto-creating the item bank when the target does not have one yet — transfers through `ISyncServerBank.transfer`, writes a `PayoutHistory` row, and advances the next run; failed one-time runs retry at a 1-second cadence instead of waiting out a full interval. Amounts are stored as scale-100 fixed point for money *and* every item currency, so fractional item payouts round-trip cleanly. A typed `PayoutFailureReason` (`INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `CURRENCY_ITEM_MISSING`, `TARGET_MISSING`, `PAUSED`, `INTERNAL`) lets interfaces localise and classify failures instead of parsing free-form text. New `PayoutHistoryManager`, `PayoutHistoryRecord` and `sql/PayoutHistory.sql` (indexed on `(company_id, ts DESC)` and `(schedule_id, ts DESC)`). New public `IPayoutManager` exposed through `BankSystemAPI.getPayoutManager()` — `createSchedule`, `updateSchedule`, `pauseSchedule`, `deleteSchedule`, `listSchedulesFor`, `getHistory`, `getTotalPaidForSchedule`. A hard floor of 20 ticks (1 second) is enforced on create and update. Every payout writes a paired `TransactionLog.PAYOUT` row on both sides, and `PayoutExecutedEvent` fires on the master for every scheduler evaluation, successful or not. Removing a worker from the company's bound bank account cascade-strips the payout schedules that targeted them. Master-only — slaves store no payout state.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Dividend distributor — one-shot, all-or-nothing payouts to every share holder, in any currency</summary><br>New master-only `DividendPayer` resolves a company's stamped-share `ItemID`, snapshots every account holding that share, precomputes `total = sum(holderShares) × amountPerShare`, verifies the company's bank can afford the entire run, and only then transfers atomically from the company account to each holder's personal bank. Insufficient funds refuse the run wholesale with no state change, and the arithmetic is overflow-safe (`Math.multiplyExact`). Dividends can be paid in **any** item the company holds — a `currencyItem` of `0` means money, non-zero selects an item. Paired `TransactionLog.DIVIDEND` rows are written per holder (outbound on the company, inbound on the holder). New public `IDividendPayer` exposed through `BankSystemAPI.getDividendPayer()` with a fail-closed slave shim. `PayDividendResult` carries a typed `Reason` (`OK`, `NOT_MASTER`, `COMPANY_MISSING`, `INVALID_INPUT`, `NO_SHARES`, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `CURRENCY_ITEM_MISSING`, `NO_PERMISSION`, `INTERNAL`) plus `totalPaid` and `holderCount`, and `DividendPaidEvent` fires on the master exactly once per successful run. Slave-initiated runs route to the master through the `PAY_DIVIDEND` request under a MANAGE gate.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Blank and Stamped Share items with company identity, live tooltip and supply tracking</summary><br>Two new items — `banksystem:blank_share` (inert; shapeless recipe of paper + leather yielding 4) and `banksystem:stamped_share`, which carries an identity-relevant integer `banksystem:company_id` data component so stamped stacks of different companies resolve to distinct `ItemID`s while stacks of the same company stay fungible. The component is registered through a new `BankSystemDataComponents` registry and is deliberately kept out of the volatile-components tag. A client-side `ShareVisualCache` (company id → visuals, issued and max supply) drives the stamped-share tooltip and is populated by three packets: `S2CCompanyVisualUpdatePacket` (broadcast on edit), `S2CCompanyVisualBulkPacket` (initial sync on login) and `S2CCompanyVisualSupplyUpdatePacket` (per stamp or redeem). The tooltip renders the display name in the configured colour, a wrapped description, and a formatted `Supply: issued / max` line; a cache miss shows a grey `Unknown Company Share` placeholder and self-heals on the next frame through a by-id lookup. `CompanyManager` gains `stampShare(int)` and `redeemShare(int)` — atomic increment and decrement of the issued counter with max-supply cap and zero floor, each broadcasting a supply update so every client stays live. `CompanyManager.updateShareVisuals(int, ShareVisuals)` is MANAGE-gated on the master, validates the chosen symbols against the server symbol library, and clamps the display name to 24 and the description to 120 characters.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Share holder index and share-trade ledger rows</summary><br>New `IBankManager.listAccountsHolding(ItemID)` (and the underlying `ISyncServerBankManager.listAccountsHolding`) returns the set of account numbers whose item bank for that `ItemID` currently holds a strictly positive total balance (free plus locked). Master-only; a slave returns an empty set. Depositing or withdrawing stamped shares now writes a `TransactionLog.SHARE_TRADE` row (populating `company_id`) instead of a plain deposit or withdrawal. New `StampedShareItem.getCompanyIdForItemID(ItemID)` resolves a company id from an `ItemID` template without an `ItemStack` round-trip.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>New public `net.kroia.banksystem.api.company` package for share visuals</summary><br>Public `ShareVisuals` value type describing a share's visual identity — a two-layer symbol design (`bgSymbolId` with `bgTint` behind `fgSymbolId` with `fgTint`), display name, description, and current plus maximum supply. `IBankSystemVisualLookup` provides client-side per-`ItemID` lookup that self-heals on a cache miss, and the optional `IShareIconRenderer` hook paints a tinted monogram badge (the first two initials, text auto-contrasted against the tint) for companies that have no symbol assigned. Both are exposed through `BankSystemAPI.getVisualLookup()` (always non-null; returns `null` per query on dedicated servers) and `BankSystemAPI.getShareIconRenderer()`. Internally each company carries a third colour — the card tint — persisted in NBT and carried through every sync path (`S2CCompanyVisualUpdatePacket`, `S2CCompanyVisualBulkPacket`, `S2SCompanyMirrorPacket`, the async request codecs, `SlaveCompanyMirror` and `ShareVisualCache`). Consumed by StockMarket's company integration to render per-share visuals in market rows, trade headers and orderbook columns without touching internals.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>Server-managed share symbol library with live client delivery</summary><br>New `ShareSymbolStore` persists share symbols as PNG files plus a `manifest.json` under `<world>/banksystem/share_symbols/`, and seeds 45 bundled 16×16 pixel-art symbols on first start — coin, scales, chest, barrel, house, tower, tree, mountain, sun, moon, bolt, anchor, hammer, axe, minecart, leaf, gear, anvil, pickaxe, sword, shield, crown, star, diamond, emerald, gold and iron ingot, wheat, apple, fish, boat, bow, potion, book, map, compass, clock, key, lantern, torch, bell, heart, skull, flame and snowflake. Server operators can add their own PNGs; removing a symbol auto-compacts ordinals so ids stay dense. The manifest is pushed to every joining player and clients pull pixel data on demand through rate-limited requests, so new symbols appear without a resource-pack reload. Five new packets ship the library to clients and mirror it to slave servers: `S2CShareSymbolManifestPacket`, `S2CShareSymbolDataPacket`, `C2SShareSymbolDataRequest`, `S2SShareSymbolManifestPacket` and `S2SShareSymbolDataPacket`. Symbol ids submitted by the visual editor are validated against the store on the master. `SharePresetRegistry` exposes `isBundledId()`, `orderedIds()` and `allIds()`, delegating dynamic-id lookups to the client symbol registry.</details> |
| ![feature] | Company description edits broadcast to every online client through the new `S2CCompanyDescriptionUpdatePacket`, so peers see the new text immediately. |
| ![bugfix] | <details><summary>Items deposited while "allow all items" was on could no longer be withdrawn</summary><br>With the `ALLOW_ALL_ITEMS` setting turned on, depositing an item that was not on the allowed-items list succeeded — but the item never joined that list. The Bank Download block builds its selection list from the allowed items, so the freshly deposited item was never offered for withdrawal, and turning the setting back off stranded the balance with no way to get it out. Every item that is given a bank slot now joins the allowed-items list, and a one-off sweep at world load adds any item that already holds a balance without being listed, so existing worlds heal themselves on the next start. Blacklisted items are still refused. Covered by a new in-game test, `banked_item_stays_allowed_after_allow_all_revert`.</details> |
| ![feature] | <details><summary>StockMarket integration — soft-dependency bridge for the share market lifecycle</summary><br>New reflection-based `StockMarketBridge` (no compile-time StockMarket imports) targets StockMarket's Market Creation API — `openMarket`, `marketExistsFor`, `closeMarket`, `setMarketOpen` and `isMarketOpen`. `AsyncCompanyManager` exposes them as requests gated on MANAGE (open, query, pause / resume) or founder (close), resolves the company's stamped-share `ItemID` through the reverse registry, and marshals the call onto the server thread. A missing StockMarket at runtime resolves cleanly to `UNAVAILABLE` (warned once, then logged at debug level), so BankSystem still boots on all three loaders without StockMarket on the classpath. Share-style market flags (`virtualOrderbookEnabled=false`, `ignorePluginAutosubscribe=true`, `naturalAbundance=0`) are enforced inside the bridge so callers cannot accidentally spawn bot-driven markets on player shares.</details> |

|---:|---|
| ![feature] | <details><summary>New `/company` command family</summary><br><ul><li><code>/company create &lt;name&gt; &lt;maxSupply&gt;</code> — creates a company and a bound multi-user bank account with the caller seeded as founder. <code>maxSupply</code> is bounded to <code>[1, 1000000000]</code>.</li><li><code>/company info &lt;companyName&gt;</code> — chat printout of company metadata (name, founders, max supply, description, issued count).</li><li><code>/company manage &lt;companyName&gt;</code> — opens the Company Management screen.</li></ul>Everything else — transferring the founder role, dissolving a company and editing the description — lives in the Company Management screen (Danger Zone and Overview tabs), and Share Stamper bindings are made by right-clicking the block, so each action has exactly one place it is done. | ![feature] | New `/banksystem symbols` administrator commands (`list`, `add`, `remove`, `reload`) — operator-only and master-gated. `add` imports a PNG through a server inbox file drop, `remove` deletes by id with automatic ordinal compaction, and `reload` re-reads the manifest from disk and pushes an updated sync to every connected client. |

## Internal

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![hardening] | <details><summary>Slave-server player joins during a down slave-to-master link are no longer silently dropped</summary><br>Previously, when a player joined a slave server before the slave-to-master handshake had completed, `AsyncBankManager.onPlayerJoinAsync` returned silently and the master never saw the join, so no default account was created for that player. Join notifications are now queued on the slave while the link is unavailable and replayed on the next accepted connection, deduplicated against currently online players so nobody is forwarded twice. Added info and debug logging on the join path, and a warning on connection failure (previously an empty callback) that surfaces the connection state.</details> |
